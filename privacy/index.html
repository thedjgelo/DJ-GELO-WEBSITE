How booking information is used

When you submit a booking inquiry, DJ GELO stores the information you provide—such as your name, email, phone number, event details and message—to respond to your request and manage a potential or confirmed booking.

This information is not sold. It may be stored using service providers used to operate the booking system and send transactional email. Only information needed to handle the inquiry or booking is collected through this form.

If you want your inquiry information corrected or deleted, email thedjgelo@gmail.com.
